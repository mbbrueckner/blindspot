### 2026-11-23, WP 2.1 / 1.3 / 3.1, Model comparison, own recordings and alert logic
- **Done:** Restructured the package (`blindspot/data`, `models`, `eval`, `alerts`, `viz`; old import paths stay valid, separate PR for Yannik). On top of it: loader for the Stray Scanner recordings with the naming scheme `<env>_<scene>_<light>_<obstacles>_<nn>` and an optional manifest `recordings.csv`; 14 depth models behind one interface `predict(rgb, intrinsics)` with a registry and groups (`tier1`, `mobile`, `camera`, ...); walking corridor and naive baseline (WP 3.1); evaluation with three alignments (raw, median, affine), errors per depth range and over distance, latency and memory, a prediction cache, and a report as Markdown and self-contained HTML with figures (later replaced by Markdown plus performance plots as PNG and PDF: accuracy vs. speed, raw vs. scaled, accuracy by distance, error over distance, day/night and indoor/outdoor; the image panels and the HTML report were dropped as not useful). One command: `uv run python scripts/run_eval.py --preset smoke` or `--preset full`. Variants now receive the intrinsics: `variant(rgb, intrinsics) -> (depth, alerts)`.
- **Worked:** All 7 Tier-1 models (MiDaS Small, DA2 Small, DA2 Metric Indoor/Outdoor Small, Metric3D v2 S, YOLO26n-Depth, ZipDepth) run on CUDA and CPU with identical results, plus all 7 Tier-2 models on CUDA (MoGe-2, UniDepthV2 and DA3 in separate environments). On one SANPO frame, only Metric3D (δ1 raw 0.989) and DA3 Metric Large (0.985) get the metric scale right without help; both use our focal length. With oracle scaling almost all reach δ1 above 0.95. Metric3D now runs without mmcv and on the CPU (two targeted fixes, see `docs/models.md`). 65 tests without torch, network or weights; Ruff clean.
- **Did not work (and why):** (1) The iPhone videos decode to one frame fewer than there are depth maps (OpenCV, PyAV and ffmpeg alike). Correlating camera rotation (odometry) with image motion showed that the *first* frame is missing (clear in 7 of 9 recordings, r = 0.88 to 0.99): video frame k belongs to depth map k+1. Without this check, image and depth would have been off by one frame. (2) MiDaS Small hung on the first run without an error: a nested `torch.hub.load` asks for trust on stdin. (3) Depth Pro is 5x too near on the SANPO test frame but correct on an iPhone frame; probably the squashing to 1536x1536 distorts the 16:9 image too much. (4) MoGe, UniDepth and DA3 do not fit into the main environment because of pinned dependencies (Python ≤ 3.13, numpy < 2, xformers); they run in separate environments and write into the same cache.
- **Numbers / measurements:** Latency on the RTX 5070 Ti (720x1280): ZipDepth 4 ms, YOLO26n 6 ms, MiDaS Small 8 ms, DA2 Small 18 to 23 ms, Metric3D S 61 ms, Depth Pro 319 ms. CPU: ZipDepth 27 ms, YOLO26n 38 ms, DA2 Small 365 ms, Metric3D S 1.2 s. Camera pitch from the ARKit poses: median per recording between −4° and +5°, so the corridor assumes 0°.
- **Full run (`results/m1_full/`):** all 14 models on 528 frames (45 SANPO, 483 from 14 own recordings, every 10th frame). The naming scheme was extended for our scenes (scene `terrace`, obstacles `furniture`, `wall`, `plant`, `door`; stairs = `step`). After alignment, predictions are clipped to 0.001 to 10 m (common NYU/KITTI protocol); before that, single pixels that Depth Pro places hundreds of meters away in front of walls dominated the mean (AbsRel 4.8 instead of 0.08).
- **Numbers, own recordings (δ1, all depths up to 10 m):** Without help (raw) no model gets the scale right: DA3 Metric L 0.86, Metric3D L 0.79, Depth Pro 0.61, Metric3D S 0.54, YOLO26n 0.39, DA2 Metric Outdoor 0.07. With median scaling per frame (close to the later ground scaling, WP 2.2): DA3 Metric L 0.93, Metric3D L 0.93, Metric3D S 0.90, UniDepthV2 S 0.90, MoGe-2 S 0.89, DA2 Metric Indoor S 0.85, YOLO26n 0.77, DA2 S 0.76, ZipDepth 0.74. With affine alignment most lie between 0.84 and 0.96. In the near range 0 to 2 m all models are worse than on average, below 1 m worst. Latency (RTX 5070 Ti): ZipDepth 4 ms, MiDaS S and YOLO26n 6 ms, DA3 S 13 ms, UniDepthV2 S 15 ms, DA2 S 18 ms, DA3 Metric L 28 ms, MoGe-2 S 47 ms, Metric3D S 65 ms, Depth Pro 333 ms, Metric3D L 366 ms.

### 2026-11-22, WP 1.4, Annotations from SANPO masks
- **Done:** SANPO has panoptic masks (class plus instance per pixel), but only for part of the real sessions and only every sixth frame is human-annotated. `download_sanpo.py --masks` loads only such sessions and frames. `blindspot/masks.py` turns each obstacle instance into a box (`scripts/make_annotations.py` writes the JSON). New test split: 4 sessions with masks, 40 frames (every 12th), replacing the hand-written draft. Split: 5 train, 1 val, 4 test sessions.
- **Decisions (to confirm with Mika):** Obstacles are people, cyclists, animals, "obstacle", vehicles, traffic signs, poles, bus stops, bike racks, trees and, since the second pass, buildings, walls/fences, barriers, handrails and traffic lights. Ground, sky and bushes/terrain do not count. `in_path` is a 2D stand-in for the walking corridor: the box overlaps the middle third of the image width. Instances below 400 mask pixels are dropped; regions without instance IDs need ten times that (4000).
- **Numbers:** 483 obstacles in 40 frames, 195 in the path. Positive frames (obstacle in the path within range): 3 m: 6, 5 m: 10, 8 m: 13, 10 m: 24, 15 m: 28 (of 40). Nearest obstacle in the path: 1.6 m. Lower bound `constant_depth`: AbsRel 0.358, RMSE 2.06 m, δ1 0.369; recall 0 at every range, as expected.
- **Did not work (and why):** With people, poles and trees only, there is not a single positive case at 3 m; all in-path obstacles were at least 6.2 m away (median 14 m), SANPO shows walkers on streets and paths. Counting walls fixes this on paper, but then all six cases under 3 m are walls or fences, and the box of a wall covers the whole connected region, so its median depth can be higher than its nearest point.
- **Open questions:** `in_path` marks a wall running along the sidewalk as in the way. The 3D corridor (WP 3.1) should decide this better; the annotation is only an approximation. Which range do we use for SANPO recall? The head-and-chest-height case for blind users exists only in our own LiDAR recordings (WP 1.3).
